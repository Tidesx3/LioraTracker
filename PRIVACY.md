# Liora privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Liora is a workout tracker made for personal use. It has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics of its own,
and it doesn't send your data to its developer or to anyone else.

## What Liora keeps, and where

Your workouts, routines, exercises, body measurements, progress photos and settings are stored on your phone,
in Liora's private storage. Progress photos never go to the phone's shared gallery.

## Google Drive (only if you turn on sync)

Liora asks for access to its own app data folder in your Google Drive (the `drive.appdata` scope) and uses it
only to keep your Liora data and progress photos in sync between your devices. The folder is hidden in Drive
and counts against your own storage. Liora can't see or change any other file in your Drive.

You can disconnect in Liora's settings. To remove Liora's data from Drive, open Google Drive on the web, go to
Settings › Manage apps, and delete Liora's hidden app data.

## Health Connect (only if you connect it)

Liora writes your finished workouts (name, start and end time, notes) to Health Connect, and reads your weight
and body fat to show them in Body. It reads nothing else. You can revoke these permissions in Health Connect
at any time.

## Files you choose

Backups and CSV exports are written only where you choose with Android's file picker, and imports read only
the file you pick.

## Exercise pictures

Pictures of the built-in exercises are downloaded from the free-exercise-db project on GitHub. These requests
carry no data about you or your training.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/Tidesx3/LioraTracker/issues>.
